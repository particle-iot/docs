## Assemble your own devices (alternative)

For small numbers of devices, or simple devices made from off-the-shelf components, you could also manufacture them yourself.

Even if you are using a custom printed circuit board, a number of companies such as JLCPCB can not only manufacturer the circuit board, but also do the pick-and-place and soldering step, making it a viable alternative for medium-sized production runs.
