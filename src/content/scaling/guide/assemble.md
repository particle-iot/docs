# {{title}}

{{section-nav}}

This section describes how your devices are assembled into your product.

{{content-nav}}

## CM assembles, tests, and flashes your devices

One option is to have your contract manufacturer (CM) assemble, test, and flash your devices. If they are able to do all of these steps for you, it is obviously the easiest solution for you and best for large deployments.

## CM assembles your devices (alternative)

It may be more feasible and less expensive to have your CM assemble the hardware, but leave the Particle platform steps to you. This is good for small to medium deployments, especially if your CM is located in China or is not familiar with the Particle platform.

## Assemble your own devices (alternative)

For small numbers of devices, or simple devices made from off-the-shelf components, you could also manufacture them yourself.

Even if you are using a custom printed circuit board, a number of companies such as JLCPCB can not only manufacturer the circuit board, but also do the pick-and-place and soldering step, making it a viable alternative for medium-sized production runs.
