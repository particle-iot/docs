Most Particle devices include a built-in Particle EtherSIM to provide connectivity for your device. The actual SIM card varies depending on the device.

**MFF2**

Most Particle devices include a MFF2 SMD EtherSIM. This SIM card is soldered to the device and cannot be replaced. There is software on the device so some carrier information can be upgraded OTA, but cannot be switched to any arbitrary carrier.

**e-sim**

The Tachyon and some devices, like the B504e, include a fully programmable e-sim. 

On the Tachyon, you are free to program your own carrier in to the SIM.

On embedded devices, while the hardware is programmable, there is no way for end-users to reprogram the SIM on devices like the B504e, so it essentially behaves the same as the MFF2 EtherSIM.

**4FF**

Some older devices include a 4FF plastic nano SIM card slot, allowing the user of a 3rd-party SIM card.

The Boron has both a 4FF SIM card slot, and a MFF2 Particle EtherSIM.
