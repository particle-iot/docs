**M.2 SoM**

M.2 SoM modules, including the B-SoM and M-SoM product lines, provide a number of cellular and Wi-Fi technologies in an interchangeable module that fits into a socket on your custom base board.

![M-SoM](/assets/images/m-series/m404-iso.jpg)

**Feather**

Feather prototyping modules, including the Photon 2 and Boron, have pins on the bottom of the module and follow the Adafruit Feather specification, allowing easy access to a large number of control, sensor, and display options with no soldering required. They can also be used in a solderless breadboard for custom designs.

![Photon 2](/assets/images/photon2-iso.jpg)

**Prototyping modules**

The older Photon 1 and Electron are also modules with bottom pins, but do not use the same pinouts as Feather devices.

**SMD modules**

There are modules that are used by SMD soldering to a custom base board instead of using a M.2 socket. These include the Tracker SoM.

![Tracker SoM](/assets/images/t523-som.svg)

The P2 module is a Wi-Fi and BLE module (not cellular) that is soldered to your own base board.

![P2](/assets/images/p2-iso.png)

The older E-Series module also is an SMD module.

**Gateway**

Gateway devices like the Tracker One and Monitor One include a complete design and an enclosure.

![Tracker One](/assets/images/at-som/at-encosure-plugged.jpg)

The Tachyon does not include an enclosure, but is also a complete, certified design.

![Tachyon](/assets/images/tachyon/tachyon-iso.jpg)
