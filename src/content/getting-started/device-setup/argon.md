# {{title}}

This page walks you through unboxing and setting up a [Argon](/reference/datasheets/wi-fi/argon-datasheet/), a Wi-Fi development board with Bluetooth LE. By the end, the Argon will be powered up and ready to add to your Particle account.

{{content-nav}}

## Unboxing

What's in the box depends on whether you purchased the Argon Kit or just the Argon device. Check the contents before you start.

### Argon Kit (ARGNKIT)

- Argon development board
- Mini breadboard
- Wi-Fi antenna (ANT-FLXV2)
- Micro-USB cable
- Starter components: two 220 Ω resistors, one red LED, and one photodiode

### Argon (ARGN-H, board only)

- Argon development board
- Wi-Fi antenna (ANT-FLXV2)

## What you'll need

- An Argon
- The Wi-Fi antenna. It is required and must be attached before you power the device.
- A micro-USB cable for power and data
- A computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux), which you'll use to set up the device and to program it with Particle Workbench
- Optionally, a Li-Po battery for portable or backup power

## Prepare the hardware

1. Connect the Wi-Fi antenna to the u.FL connector on the Argon. Be sure to use the Wi-Fi connector, not the BT (bluetooth) connector.
2. If you want to use a battery, connect the Li-Po battery.
3. Connect the micro-USB cable between the Argon and your computer. This provides both power and a data connection.

## Add to your account

To add the device to your account and make sure it's running the latest Device OS, go to [setup.particle.io](https://setup.particle.io/). The guided setup will:

- Sign you in, or create a Particle account if you do not have one yet
- Allow you to select a Wi-Fi network to connect to
- Update the Device OS if necessary

## Next steps

Once setup is complete, continue with the [getting started guide](/getting-started/guide/device-setup/) to explore the Particle console and program your device.
